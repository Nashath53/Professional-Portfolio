About

A short version of me

I'm a web developer who builds sites for small studios, tutors, and startups — from the first line of HTML to a site that's live, responsive, and easy for someone else to maintain. I work close to the code: no page builders, no bloated frameworks beyond what a project actually needs.

Outside client work, I write about front-end patterns and keep a running archive of layout and interaction details worth reusing.
